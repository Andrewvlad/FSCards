# USPA SCM revisions

Only Ch.9 (Formation Skydiving) and Ch.7 (Collegiate) are watched and compared, since the USPA image sets are cut from those two chapters.

Newest first. A month lists the chapters revised that month, each compared with its previous edition. Formations lists each pool's renamed, removed, added and moved formations by key, naming a block by its first and last formation. Rules diffs the rest of the text: `-` lines are from the earlier edition, `+` lines from this one, and `(N)` is the PDF page number. Renumbered rules, rewrapped lines, page headers and footers, the table of contents and the formation pages are left out.
